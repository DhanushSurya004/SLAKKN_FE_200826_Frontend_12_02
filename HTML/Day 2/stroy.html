My Love for Cricket

The Beginning

As a child, I was never really interested in sports, especially cricket.

But on May 2, 2013, I went to Chepauk to watch CSK vs Punjab, and that day changed everything.

The Match That Changed Everything

I watched Suresh Raina score an incredible 100.

The atmosphere of the stadium, the crowd, and his brilliant batting made me fall in love with cricket for the first time.


From that day, cricket became more than just a sport to me, and Raina became my favourite player.

My Journey as a Cricket Fan

I started watching more cricket matches and slowly learned more about the game, its players, and its strategies.

I also started supporting CSK passionately and enjoyed watching their matches with my family and friends.

Cricket became an important part of my life.

A Special Memory

Looking back, that one match was the beginning of my journey as a cricket fan.

I went to the stadium without much interest in cricket and came back with a love for the game that has stayed with me ever since.
